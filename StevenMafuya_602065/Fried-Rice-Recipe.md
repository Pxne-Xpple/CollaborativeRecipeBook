\# Fried Rice



A quick, flavorful dish that is a fantastic way to utilize leftover rice!



\### Ingredients

* 3 cups Cooked Rice (day-old preferred)
* 3 Eggs
* 1 cup Diced Vegetables (onion, carrot, string beans, etc.)
* 2 tbsp Olive Oil
* 1/3 cup Soy Sauce
* 1/3 cup Water



\### Directions

1. Sauté Vegetables: Heat the olive oil in a pan or wok over medium-high heat. Add the diced vegetables and sauté until tender.

2\. Combine Rice: Toss the cooked rice and water into the pan, stirring consistently to break up clumps.

3\. Cook Eggs: In a separate pan, scramble the eggs completely, then toss them into the main mixture.

4\. Season and Serve: Pour in the soy sauce, stir thoroughly to combine all elements, and serve hot!
